RoleClue supports a short guest onboarding session, matching against current Australian technology listings, and private application preparation. The Stripe section below applies when you choose a one-time purchase or subscription.

Who this notice covers

This notice covers the RoleClue member app at app.proairesis.digital, including registration and resume upload. It is separate from the marketing website and introductory-booking service.

RoleClue is operated by PROAIRESIS DIGITAL, ABN 71 151 568 558, at 49 Furzer St, Phillip ACT 2606, Australia. Contact privacy@proairesis.digital for privacy matters or support@proairesis.digital for general support.

What you provide and why

You can start before creating an account. We issue a random guest token and temporarily store the situation, resume text, confirmed profile, results and decisions needed to continue. The browser stores the token, not the resume or job description. An unclaimed guest record expires within 24 hours. Do not share the token.

We collect your name and verified email for account access. Amazon Cognito handles your password, verification and sign-in. RoleClue uses the account identifier and sign-in tokens to keep each account's records separate.

Before account creation, we process the uploaded PDF or DOCX to extract text and skills, but we do not keep the original guest file. If you upload a resume later from the member console, we store that original file temporarily as described below. Your profile includes the experience, job-search situation, work-rights and sponsorship answers, role interests and optional preferences or practical limitations that you choose to supply. These support job matching and application drafts. We do not infer visa status, work rights, sponsorship or other sensitive personal characteristics from your resume. Do not provide visa numbers or identity documents.

The app also stores your profile confirmation and consent choices, opportunity decisions, application status and notes, and saved drafts when you use those features. The optional product-update preference is separate from accepting the terms; you can change it in My profile. Only include personal information you want processed, and avoid identity-document numbers or other unnecessary sensitive details.

Where information is processed

The app uses AWS in Sydney (ap-southeast-2): Cognito for account access, S3 for private resume files, DynamoDB for account records and Lambda for processing. CloudFront delivers the website through a global network. Network delivery and provider support or operations can involve processing outside Australia; we do not promise that every technical operation remains in Sydney.

Resume files and account records are not published as public profiles. Technical request information supports delivery, security and troubleshooting. App logging is designed to avoid resume text, draft text, passwords and sign-in tokens.

When you explicitly request an AI cover letter and confirm the processing acknowledgment, we send your reviewed resume text, relevant profile fields and the selected job description to OpenAI's API. The original resume file is not sent. Processing may occur outside Australia. We request that OpenAI not store the response as application state; this is not a zero-retention guarantee. OpenAI's default abuse monitoring can retain content for up to 30 days, with exceptions for legal or safety needs. Its API data is not used for model training by default. See OpenAI's data controls.

For live discovery, RoleClue sends bounded role and Australia filters to the public Freehire job-search API. It does not send your name, resume, visa answer or other profile content to Freehire. RoleClue then ranks the returned public listings against your confirmed profile on our service and links you to the original employer application page. Freehire and the listing's underlying source are identified in the result where available.

Pasted job descriptions and source links stay within your account and are used for matching and requested drafts. RoleClue does not use your LinkedIn or SEEK login, scrape those accounts or submit an application for you. Only submit text you are permitted to use.

Plan and payment information

If you open enabled checkout, we send Stripe your verified account email, internal account identifier and checkout terms acceptance time/version. Stripe collects the billing and payment information you provide through its hosted Checkout and Customer Portal. RoleClue does not store your full card number or security code, or send your resume, job descriptions or cover letters to Stripe.

We store Stripe customer, purchase and subscription references; payment and subscription status; paid-period dates; Starter and monthly generation usage; and checkout consent records. We verify Stripe's payment, cancellation and refund information to maintain the correct allowance, enforce one Starter purchase per account, avoid duplicate billing and provide support. App deletion does not automatically delete Stripe's payment records.

Stripe processes data for payments, fraud prevention and compliance. Processing can occur outside Australia, including in the United States and India. Stripe may retain financial records for its legal, tax, accounting and fraud-prevention obligations. Its privacy notice describes this processing, retention and your choices: Stripe Privacy Policy.

Browser storage and measurement

The app stores sign-in tokens and pending-generation request identifiers in your browser's session storage. Signing out clears that app session storage. The member app does not currently implement advertising trackers or optional analytics. The separate marketing website has its own technology notice.

How long records remain

Configured retention for this member app
Record Retention
Unclaimed guest records The extracted resume text, confirmed profile, matches and decisions expire within 24 hours. The original guest resume file is not kept.
Original resume files Member-console uploads have S3 lifecycle expiry after seven days. Pending member-console uploads expire after one day. Expiry schedules removal; AWS deletion is not instantaneous.
Extracted resume and account records Kept for your account until you delete it, including your reviewed profile, decisions, applications and saved drafts.
App and API logs Configured 30-day log retention.
Database recovery history A seven-day point-in-time recovery window. Deleted records can remain in recovery history until that window expires.
Billing and payment records Operational billing references are held while needed to manage your account. Payment, invoice, refund and related financial records may remain with Stripe and in our business records where needed for accounting, legal obligations or payment disputes. They are not all removed by app account deletion. Contact us about access or deletion of those records.
Deleted-account protection A one-way hash of a deleted account's internal identifier is retained to prevent an already-issued sign-in token from reopening that account. This record does not contain your name, email or resume and has no scheduled expiry.

Access, correction and deletion

Use My profile to view and correct your details. Account provides an export of stored account data and a deletion control. Deletion removes operational resume, profile, application and draft records and the sign-in identity, subject to the recovery window above.

A recent upload link can delay deletion for up to seven minutes after the latest upload starts. The deletion control also waits for pending generation or checkout operations and requires a subscription that can still bill to end. Cancelling renewal at period end does not end the subscription immediately. A refund alone also does not cancel it. Use Account → Manage subscription or contact support if billing or an unfinished operation prevents deletion. Financial records described above may remain after the app account is deleted.

For access, correction, deletion or a privacy concern, email privacy@proairesis.digital. We may need to verify that the request concerns your account. You can also consult the OAIC's information about access requests or contact the OAIC about a privacy complaint. This notice does not restrict applicable legal rights.
